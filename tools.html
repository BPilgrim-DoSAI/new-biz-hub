01 — The Tools

Your AI Toolkit

Miroma's approved AI platforms, with everything you need to use them well. Launch any tool directly or work through the training to get up to speed. Anything not on this list isn't authorised for use without prior approval.

01

Claude

AI Assistant

The most capable AI assistant available, and the one you'll use most. Claude can write, research, analyse data, summarise long documents, draft presentations, review contracts and automate repetitive work — all through conversation. Available on web, mobile, desktop and inside Word, Excel and PowerPoint.

Writing & Editing Research Data Analysis Strategy Code Long Documents

02

LTX Studio

AI Video Generation

Miroma's approved platform for AI image and video generation. Use LTX Studio to produce concept visuals, storyboards, social content, campaign mockups and short videos — straight from a script or brief. No production team or specialist skills required.

AI Video Storyboarding Script to Screen Social Content Campaign Video
Launch → Training

03

Descript

Audio & Video Editing

The fastest way to edit audio and video. Descript transcribes your recording automatically — then you edit the media by editing the text. Cut filler words, remove sections, reorder content and export to Premiere or Final Cut. Ideal for podcasts, client videos, webinar clips and social repurposing.

Auto-Transcription Filler Word Removal Podcast Editing Video Repurposing Screen Recording
Launch → Training

04

Springboards

Creative Ideation

An AI platform for advertising and marketing teams who want better ideas, not just faster answers. Springboards generates a wide range of creative directions, angles and territories from any brief — helping teams explore more ground, pressure-test concepts, and push into unexpected territory before committing to a direction. Use it solo or share a session with your team in real time.

Creative Ideation Concept Development Brief Exploration Team Brainstorming Creative Strategy
Launch → Training

05

Fireflies

Meeting Intelligence

An AI meeting assistant that joins your calls automatically. Fireflies records, transcribes and summarises every meeting — surfacing key decisions, action items and follow-ups without anyone needing to take notes. Search across all your past meetings and share highlights with your team in one click.

Transcription Action Items Meeting Search AI Summaries CRM Integration
Launch → Training

06

Gemini

AI Assistant · Google Workspace

Google Enterprise only

Google's AI assistant, built directly into the apps your team already uses. Gemini works inside Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive and Meet — letting you draft emails, summarise documents, analyse data, build presentations and generate content without switching platforms.

Available to teams operating under a Google Enterprise agreement only. Under Enterprise tier, your data stays within Google's secure infrastructure and is governed by your existing contractual terms, making it suitable for day-to-day work within our Data Use Policy. All usage must follow our standard tiered data approach — identify your data tier before use and apply the required controls. Full policy on the Policy & Guidelines page. If you're unsure whether your team is on Enterprise tier, check with your IT lead before use.
